ABOUT FLIP-LAB

Not fast fashion.
Not a thrift pile.

flip-lab exists somewhere in between.

We search for jackets with character, inspect them for wear, clean them properly, and put them back into circulation.

Some are old. Some are forgotten. Some simply deserve another owner.

The goal is simple: find something worth keeping.

THE FLIP-LAB PROCESS

Four steps.

01

Hunt

We search through secondhand sources for pieces with potential.

02

Check

Each jacket is inspected for condition, construction, damage, sizing, and overall character.

03

Flip

Pieces are cleaned, prepared, photographed, and listed.

04

Pass It On

One jacket leaves the collection and begins another life with someone else.
